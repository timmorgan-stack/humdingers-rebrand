Light bites, grazing platters and bowl food that travel well and taste even better in the sun, plus a full drinks and cocktail service to match.

Why Humdingers

Seasonal, made from scratch

Everything is cooked in-house from fresh, locally sourced ingredients — never pre-made, never compromised on flavour.

An informal chat, not a form

Every booking starts with a conversation about what you actually need, not a rigid package.

Every booking gives back

Profit from our Catering & Events work helps fund our Community Kitchen, serving 1,000+ free meals a week.

Where we cater

We cover London as a whole, with particular focus on Central London, the City, Canary Wharf, Westminster, Mayfair, Soho, Holborn, Fitzrovia, King's Cross, Shoreditch, Hoxton, Islington and Clerkenwell.

For larger events such as weddings and private celebrations, we're happy to travel beyond the M25 — generally up to around two hours from our Hoxton kitchen, so we can guarantee the quality and freshness of the food. Somewhere else in mind? Just ask.
