Weddings

More ways we help

Seminars & Conferences

We help you find a venue, book staff, and provide the right catering and drinks for you and your attendees — coordinating the lot so you can focus on the agenda.

Drinks & Cocktails

An all-inclusive bar, alcoholic or not, plus premium hot drinks from friends like The London Tea Makers, and wine pairing advice from our own Wine Store.

Bar Hire

From a cash bar to pre-paid, quirky to classically corporate — we work with trusted suppliers to build the bar that suits your event.

Staffing

A reliable roster of brand ambassadors, mixologists, waiting staff, cloakroom and supervisors — so your event runs effortlessly from start to finish.

Location & Studio Catering

Breakfast, lunch and individually packaged bento boxes for shoots and studios — built for hygiene and easy on-set logistics.

A La Carte

Traditional and popular dishes, freshly made with the finest seasonal ingredients, adapted to suit whatever occasion you're planning.
