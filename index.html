Why choose us

Why choose Humdingers Catering London?

Our catering menus are seasonal and, wherever possible, we make everything in-house from scratch with fresh, locally sourced ingredients. We keep up with what's current, but don't pander to trends. Our food is beautifully presented & unique, and we never compromise on flavour. We see joy in every part of life and bring a revelry to our cooking. We believe food should be enjoyed; our portions are generous, and our food is well-seasoned and big on flavour. Our team of chefs come from a diverse background, and these varying influences continuously shape our menus. We're always on the lookout for new ideas and concepts and we love fulfilling our clients' wide-ranging requests.

Rooted in Hoxton

Outstanding catering London

Humdingers is an independent caterer and event planner rooted in the heart of Hoxton. We craft high-end, unforgettable food experiences for weddings, private parties, location catering, corporate gatherings, banquets, and award ceremonies. Whether you want complete, hands-on support from the very first spark of an idea, or just expert guidance in the days, weeks, or months leading up to your event, we'll create the perfect catering and events package tailored to your desires. Events catering in London has never felt this effortless—or this irresistible. Every enquiry begins with an intimate conversation. We listen closely to your vision, your tastes, and your ambitions. For us, truly knowing the people we work with is the secret ingredient that turns a great event into something exceptional.
