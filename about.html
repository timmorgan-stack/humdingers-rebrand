Our story

Our founder Robert Hunningher started Humdingers with a simple vision: bringing unpretentious but delicious food to as many people as possible. After years honing his craft in some of London's best professional kitchens, he broke out on his own with a cake stall in Stoke Newington, selling homemade cakes to hungry locals.

Within six months he'd moved to a larger kitchen to start a sandwich and salad delivery service for local NHS walk-in centres. A year later he transformed a thousand-square-foot space within Rosemary Works in Hoxton into a fully equipped kitchen — and Humdingers Catering began.

Five years on, growing success meant Humdingers needed a bigger home, and in 2011 it moved to Hoxton Street. The business has since catered weddings, events, location shoots, fashion houses and corporate companies across London.

When the pandemic hit in March 2020, Robert recognised the needs of the local community. With events on hold, the Humdingers kitchen turned its attention to feeding people who needed it most — delivering over 1,200 hot, nutritious meals a week to NHS staff on Covid-19 wards at the Royal London Hospital, and raising funds to keep that going. Robert's work through the pandemic was recognised with a British Empire Medal in 2020.

That effort grew into the Community Kitchen, which has since served over 100,000 free meals to the local neighbourhood. Humdingers Catering continues to provide catering for its clients, while giving back to the community that helped build it — every booking helps fund the next free meal.

Doing it responsibly

Sustainability, built into how we cook.

Seasonal & local

Menus are built around seasonal ingredients, sourced from local suppliers wherever possible. Several mornings a week, Robert visits London's wholesale markets himself to choose the best produce available.

Zero waste

We prepare food to order wherever possible to minimise waste, and any surplus is donated through our Community Kitchen — so nothing goes to waste.

Responsible packaging

We work with Envior on recyclable, compostable and biodegradable packaging wherever practical, and encourage reusable serviceware at staffed events.

Ethical suppliers

We choose suppliers who share our values and commitment to ethical sourcing, such as Volcano Coffee Works.

Lower-emission delivery

The majority of our delivery fleet is already electric, and we're working towards transitioning the rest as part of our long-term sustainability strategy.

Working with others

We recently took part in a sustainability event with our partner venue, Spacemade, bringing together like-minded businesses to share ideas and improve our environmental impact.
