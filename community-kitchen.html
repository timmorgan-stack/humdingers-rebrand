How it started

When the pandemic hit in March 2020, our kitchen was suddenly quiet — so our chefs put their talent to good use instead. At the peak, Humdingers delivered 1,200 hot, nutritious meals a week to NHS staff on Covid-19 wards at the Royal London Hospital, and raised funds to keep it going. That effort never really stopped; it grew into the Community Kitchen you see today.

How it works

Everyone is welcome — our aim is simply to feed as many people as we can who need a hot meal. Any profit made by our Catering & Events work goes straight back into funding the Community Kitchen, so every booking you make helps someone eat tonight.
