Why choose full-service

We handle the details, you enjoy the moment

End-to-end coordination

From initial brief through to post-event cleanup. Venue coordination, staffing, timing, logistics — we make it seamless.

Your own team on the day

Professional, reliable staff who know how to move through an event effortlessly. Brand ambassadors, waiters, supervisors — whoever you need.

Bespoke menus, no compromise

Work with our head chef to create menus that fit your vision. Themed, traditional, innovative — we adapt to your brief, not the other way around.

What we do

The full Humdingers service

Weddings

From intimate ceremonies to large celebrations. Bespoke menus, coordinated timings, discreet service that lets you enjoy your day.

Corporate events

Conferences, product launches, client entertainment — we manage catering so your team can focus on the agenda.

Private dining

Dinner parties, anniversaries, milestone celebrations — hosted at your home or a private venue, with full table service.

Drinks & cocktails

All-inclusive bar service, premium mixologists, or wine pairing advice — we work with trusted suppliers to build the perfect bar.

Venue sourcing

Can't decide on a space? We work with trusted venues across London and help you find the perfect fit for your event.

Event planning

We manage the moving parts — timings, staff coordination, setup, service flow, breakdown — so your event runs effortlessly.

How we work with you

1. Informal chat

We start with a conversation, not a form. You tell us your vision, your guest count, your venue, your brief.

2. Bespoke proposal

We put together a tailored quote covering food, staffing, drinks, any special requirements. Full transparency, no surprises.

3. Seamless delivery

Our team arrives early, sets up, runs your event flawlessly, and cleans up. You focus on your guests.
