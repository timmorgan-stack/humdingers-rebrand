The occasion

An autumnal wedding with 140 guests at the stunning Round Chapel in Clapton.

The custom menu

A selection of canapés for the guests to enjoy on the last of the warm(ish) afternoons of the year, while the room was turned around.

Into a sharing style buffet including roast lamb, boulangere potatoes, pumpkin with ricotta, butterbeans and kale.

Ending with luxurious salted caramel chocolate pots with Manuka honeycomb — always a crowd pleaser!

The drinks

A flowing bar with cask ales & plenty of gin!

“Thanks so much for all you did to make our wedding day go without a hitch. We've had so many people pass on how great you were — the photographer, florist, best man & bridesmaids… the guests too, especially the vegans & gluten free — thanks for looking after them so well! We really appreciate all your hard work on the day, you went above and beyond.” — Fiona & Chris
