The occasion

A summer ceremony at Wimborne House followed by breakfast for 130 guests, then onto another East End venue for the after party.

The custom menu

Canapé classics including seabass ceviche, smoked duck doughnuts and butternut squash arancini.

Followed by a feast of Moroccan lamb, sweet potato, Swiss chard & goat's cheese gratin, wilted greens and smoky chickpeas with chilli & lemon tahini, and roasted turmeric cauliflower with pomegranate & lemon yoghurt.

A split dessert of salted caramel chocolate pots with Manuka honeycomb, and lemon tarts with crushed meringue & fresh raspberries.

The drinks

A fabulous evening party with a full bar and lots of bubbles, thrown at Bethnal Green Working Men's Club with classic pie & mash to finish the day!

“We used Humdingers as caterers for our wedding at Wimborne House in July and had the best day. Everything ran better than we could have ever imagined, and working with Silvia and the team on the day was effortless. The food was outstanding — we've had so many great comments about everything from canapés to lamb to dessert. Humdingers were some of the most dependable people in the whole planning process. We wholeheartedly recommend.” — Jack & James
