Where we cater

We cover London as a whole, with particular focus on Central London, the City, Canary Wharf, Westminster, Mayfair, Soho, Holborn, Fitzrovia, King's Cross, Shoreditch, Hoxton, Islington and Clerkenwell.

For larger events such as weddings and private celebrations, we're happy to travel beyond the M25 — generally up to around two hours from our Hoxton kitchen, so we can guarantee the quality and freshness of the food. Somewhere else in mind? Just ask.
