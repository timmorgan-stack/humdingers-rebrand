How it works

Simple, flexible, seasonal

Pick a menu

Choose from 14 seasonally-changing menus — breakfast, sandwiches, salads, canapés, hot meals and more. All items are adjustable for dietary requirements.

Tell us when

Let us know your guest count, date and delivery location. We'll confirm availability and coordinate drop-off at your venue or office.

We deliver ready to serve

Fresh, beautifully presented food arrives in insulated boxes, ready to plate and serve. No heating, no fuss, just impress your guests.
