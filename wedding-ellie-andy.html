The occasion

Just shy of 100 guests turned up to celebrate a mid-summer wedding at Wimborne House for an all-day long extravaganza!

The custom menu

A selection of beautiful canapés including roasted pork belly & queen scallop skewers, lobster tempura, and beetroot, carrot & kale fritters with coconut yoghurt.

Followed by a plentiful spread of spring lamb & Mediterranean vegetable lasagne, with sides of tenderstem broccoli and smoky chickpeas with harissa vinaigrette.

Finished off with a summery classic Humdingers version of an Eton mess — and bagels all round for the late-night snack, a true East End classic!

The drinks

A full bar including all the typical tastes — lots & lots of their favourite gin!

“My wife and I used Humdingers for our wedding in August 2017, and we couldn't have been happier with our choice. Niall and his team supplied canapés and then a full buffet, and everything was delicious, freshly prepared and clearly made with great care and attention. On top of the food, Humdingers also took care of many of the organisation and preparation tasks for the venue on the day. I'd be very surprised if anyone was disappointed by choosing Humdingers as their wedding caterer.” — Ellie & Andy
